FAQ

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawaal

Aakash Jaipur mein admission kaise milta hai?
Aakash Jaipur mein admission ke liye aapko registration form fill karna hota hai jiske baad ek counselling session hoti hai. Kuch courses mein Aakash National Admission Test (ANAT) dena hota hai jisme scholarship bhi milti hai.
Kya scholarship available hai?
Haan! Aakash mein merit-based scholarships available hain. ANAT test mein jo students top rank laate hain unhe 10% se lekar 90% tak ki scholarship milti hai. Financial need-based scholarship ke liye bhi apply kar sakte hain.
Batch size kitna hota hai?
Aakash Jaipur mein maximum 40 students per batch rakhte hain taaki har student ko personal attention mile. Kuch premium batches mein yeh limit aur bhi kam hoti hai — 25-30 students per batch.
Kya online classes bhi available hain?
Haan, Aakash Digital Program ke through live + recorded classes available hain. Aakash app se aap kisi bhi device par classes access kar sakte hain. Study material, tests sab kuch online milta hai.
Fees refund policy kya hai?
Admission ke pehle 15 din mein agar koi genuine karan hai toh partial refund ki facility available hai (terms & conditions apply). Detail ke liye counsellor se directly baat karein.
Doubt clearing kab hoti hai?
Regular doubt sessions har subject mein weekly hote hain. Iske alawa Aakash app par 24/7 doubt clearing chatbot available hai. Special doubt sessions exam time par extra schedule kiye jaate hain.
Study material provide kiya jaata hai?
Haan, fee mein complete study material included hai jisme theory books, DPP sheets, practice papers, previous year questions, aur formula sheets sab shamil hain. Digital version bhi app par available hai.
Parent-teacher meetings kab hoti hain?
Monthly PTM schedule ki jaati hai jisme parents apne child ki progress ke baare mein directly teachers se baat kar sakte hain. Emergency mein appointment lekar bhi aa sakte hain.
Jaipur mein Aakash ke kitne centres hain?
Jaipur mein currently 4 Aakash centres hain: MI Road (Main), Mansarovar, Vaishali Nagar, aur Malviya Nagar. Sabse nazdiki centre ki information ke liye helpline par call karein.
EMI facility available hai?
Haan! Fee ko 3, 6, ya 12 monthly installments mein bhi pay kar sakte hain. Easy EMI options 0% interest par available hain. Details ke liye counsellor se baat karein.
